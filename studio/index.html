Orumi Studio

A visual music language made of colored squares.

What is Orumi?

Orumi is an open-source visual music language where sound is represented through color, shape, position, and structure.

What Orumi Studio Builds

Orumi Studio develops the language, tools, games, physical blocks, and creative experiments that help people explore music visually.

The Orumi Principle

Orumi should be simple at the surface and deep underneath. A child should be able to play with it, and a musician should be able to build complex structure with it.

The Vision

Orumi Studio imagines a world where composing music can feel like building, drawing, playing, and discovering.
