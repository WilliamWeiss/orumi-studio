Glossary

Every term introduced across the Learning Path, in one place. New terms are added here as new lessons introduce them.

Structure & Arrangement
Verse
Sets the scene. In older songs, this is the part that arrives first and tells a little story before the tune everyone actually remembers shows up. In other arrangements, "Verse" or "Bridge" can instead appear in the middle of a piece, functioning as a Transition away from and back to the main tune. Introduced in Structure — Lesson 1
Chorus
The hook. The melody people can hum without ever having sung the song — it tends to come back more than once, sometimes with small changes each time. Introduced in Structure — Lesson 1
Tag
A few bars at the very end that repeat something already sung — often the last line, sometimes a phrase borrowed from earlier — before the final chord is allowed to fully land. Barbershoppers sing these on their own, apart from the song they came from. Introduced in Structure — Lesson 1
